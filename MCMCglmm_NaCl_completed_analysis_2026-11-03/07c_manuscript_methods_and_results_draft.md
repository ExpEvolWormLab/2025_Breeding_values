# Among-RIL covariance: manuscript-ready draft (subject to model sensitivity)

## Methods
We estimated the covariance structure among seven NaCl phenotypes (SF, SB, FS, FB, BS, BF and female body area) in the CeMEE recombinant inbred lines with a multivariate Gaussian mixed model in MCMCglmm 2.30. Phenotypes were standardized for numerical stability. Fixed effects comprised trait-specific intercepts and trait-specific effects of logD, relative humidity and temperature; random effects included an unstructured among-RIL (`pop_label`) covariance matrix, trait-specific date and session components, and an unstructured residual covariance. The same seven-trait model was fitted in three independent MCMC chains with 300,000 iterations, 50,000 burn-in iterations and thinning of 125, yielding 2,000 retained draws per chain. The analysis used proper inverse-Wishart covariance priors with `nu = 8` for each 7-dimensional covariance matrix (`V = 0.25 I` for the RIL component and `V = 0.65 I` for the residual component, with `V = 0.08 I` for nuisance components). The fitted matrix describes among-RIL covariance and should not automatically be equated with an additive genetic G-matrix. The unstructured RIL and residual covariance blocks returned 49 ordered matrix entries each, reconstructed and verified as symmetric.

## Results
All 112 reported covariance/variance terms converged adequately under the run specification (maximum Gelman-Rubin Rhat = 1.0030; minimum effective sample size = 5,383 across three chains). The 6,000 reconstructed posterior covariance draws were symmetric and positive definite and reproduced the provided model-exported covariance and correlation matrices numerically. Many of the seven traits exhibited marked among-RIL correlations, including SF–SB (r = 0.694; 95% posterior interval 0.625–0.751), FS–BS (r = 0.679; 0.589–0.753), FB–BF (r = 0.576; 0.472–0.658), and BF–female area (r = −0.382; −0.499 to −0.257). Of the 21 pairwise correlations, 18 had marginal 95% posterior intervals excluding zero. This count is descriptive rather than adjusted for multiple comparisons.

The eigenvalues of the posterior mean among-RIL correlation matrix were approximately 3.405, 1.268, 0.885, 0.585, 0.551, 0.245 and 0.062. Its leading axis explained 48.6% of the summed correlation-matrix eigenvalues (posterior mean 48.8%, 95% interval 44.0–53.3%). Using the prespecified effective-dimensionality measure `Neff = n − mean((λ_i−1)^2)` for `n = 7` traits, the estimate based on the posterior mean correlation matrix was 5.901; its posterior mean was 5.879 (95% interval 5.641–6.104). For clarity, the alternative eigenvalue participation-ratio dimensionality had posterior mean 3.312 (95% interval 2.968–3.692), and should not be conflated with Neff.

As a conditional quantitative-genetic projection, we transformed the among-RIL covariance to standardized RIL BLUE units and multiplied by the estimated seven-trait selection-gradient vector. The cosine between the fitted gradient and the `G_RIL β` response direction was 0.824 at the mean covariance and 0.820 across covariance posterior draws (95% interval 0.748–0.888). These values condition on the estimated fitness gradient; its uncertainty was **not** propagated, and this projection is not itself an independently validated forecast of evolution in the selected populations.
